## Repository Overview

This repository hosts the **R scripts** and data for analyzing 
**broad-scale biogeographic patterns of phenotypic plasticity** 
across 20 functional traits of the cosmopolitan grass 
*Phragmites australis*. The workflow covers data cleaning and 
missing-value imputation, computation of the plasticity index, linear 
mixed-model analyses of latitudinal and continental structure 
while accounting for genetic lineage, climate–plasticity modeling, and multivariate 
clustering to identify plasticity syndromes.

For comprehensive results and methodological details, please 
refer to our study:

> **"Biogeography of multidimensional phenotypic plasticity and 
> its syndromes in a cosmopolitan plant species."**
